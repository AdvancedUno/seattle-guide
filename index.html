Welcome to Seattle!

Seattle is my favorite city in the Pacific Northwest. It sits right on Puget Sound with views of Mount Rainier on clear days, blending city life with the outdoors. It is also famous as the birthplace of grunge music and home to the original Starbucks.

Whether you want to try local coffee, hike through evergreen forests, or explore waterfront piers, it is an ideal city for a weekend getaway.

The Space Needle was built for the 1962 World's Fair. Read more about its history on Wikipedia.

Why You Should Go

A lot of people think it rains non-stop in Washington, but summers here are sunny, dry, and mild. Here are three big reasons to visit:

  • Coffee Culture: Independent roasters and neighborhood cafes sit on almost every corner.
  • Quick Access to Nature: You can paddleboard on Lake Union in the morning and hike the Cascades by afternoon.
  • Fresh Seafood: Local wild salmon, Dungeness crab, and hot clam chowder are local staples.

Cool Spots to Check Out

If it is your first trip, visit Pike Place Market to watch the fishmongers throw fish, and walk through the historic lower levels. Neighborhoods like Capitol Hill and Fremont offer local vintage shops, bookstores, and street art.

Taking a Washington State Ferry ride to Bainbridge Island is also an easy, low-cost way to get panoramic skyline views directly from the water.
